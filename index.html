FAQ

Will this generate revenue?

Maybe, maybe not. Currently, the primary objectives are learning and real-world impact. Some products could potentially generate revenue, but the community hasn't decided whether this is important or how finances would be handled. Potentially, some or all revenue could be funneled back into this open-source movement as an NGO.

Will the tools & projects we produce be open source?

Open question — the community is still figuring this out. Pull requests and discussion are welcome!

What's the incentive to share ideas with the community?

A few reasons: gather more project members, traffic, users, and funding; get advice and help on building; and learn faster by teaching others. We're still thinking through this — join the discussion!
